What predicts 90-day readmission?

Multivariable logistic regression forest plot with 95% CIs. Hover over any point for exact odds ratios, confidence intervals, and p-values.

Depressive disorders (OR 1.78), diagnostic complexity (OR 1.31/category), and physical restraint use (OR 1.58) emerged as significant — while ACE score, C-SSRS flags, and length of stay did not.

03

The limits of prediction

Apparent vs. bootstrap-corrected vs. out-of-fold AUC for logistic regression, random forest, and CatBoost. Visualizes the optimism inflation common in small clinical datasets.

CatBoost apparent AUC 0.90 collapsed to 0.64 OOF — worse than logistic regression. PPV ≈ 20% at the optimal threshold rules out individual triage.

Readmission risk across the diagnostic spectrum

Ranked bar chart of 90-day readmission rates for every DSM-5 category in the cohort (n ≥ 20). Wilson 95% CIs shown. Hover for patient counts and raw numbers.

Highest rates in tic disorders (21.6%) and other neurodevelopmental conditions; lowest in adjustment and somatic categories (<7%).
